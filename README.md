## Ecommerce Platforms

- [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com) - Complete commerce platform for selling online, in-store, and everywhere in between.
- [WooCommerce](https://woocommerce.com) - Open-source ecommerce plugin built for WordPress.
- [Magento](https://magento.com) - Open-source ecommerce platform with flexible architecture for large catalogs.
- [BigCommerce](https://www.bigcommerce.com) - Scalable SaaS ecommerce solution for fast-growing brands.
- [PrestaShop](https://www.prestashop.com) - Free, open-source ecommerce platform with a large module ecosystem.
- [OpenCart](https://www.opencart.com) - Self-hosted, open-source storefront platform with 13,000+ modules.
- [Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com) - Website builder with built-in commerce for smaller online stores.
- [Wix](https://www.wix.com) - Website builder offering Wix Stores for ecommerce and multichannel selling.

## Payments

- [Stripe](https://stripe.com) - Developer-friendly payment processing platform for online businesses.
- [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com) - Global digital payment processor and checkout standard for online stores.
- [Square](https://squareup.com) - Payment processing and point-of-sale system for online and in-person sales.
- [Adyen](https://www.adyen.com) - Enterprise payments platform supporting 150+ currencies and local methods.
- [Klarna](https://www.klarna.com) - Buy-now-pay-later payments provider popular with fashion and lifestyle brands.
- [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com) - Merchant-of-record payment platform that handles sales tax and VAT for digital products.
- [Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com) - Merchant-of-record checkout for selling digital products and licenses.

## Shipping & Fulfillment

- [ShipStation](https://www.shipstation.com) - Multichannel shipping and order-management platform for ecommerce.
- [Shippo](https://goshippo.com) - Shipping API and dashboard for discounted labels and tracking across carriers.
- [Easyship](https://www.easyship.com) - Global shipping platform with discounted rates and customs handling.
- [EasyPost](https://www.easypost.com) - Shipping API for label generation, rate comparison, and tracking.

## Inventory Management

- [inFlow](https://www.inflowinventory.com) - Inventory and order management software for product-based businesses.
- [Cin7](https://www.cin7.com) - Cloud inventory and order management platform for omnichannel sellers.

## Marketplaces

- [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com) - Largest online marketplace with FBA fulfillment for third-party sellers.
- [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com) - Marketplace for handmade, vintage, and craft supplies.
- [eBay](https://www.ebay.com) - Global auction and fixed-price marketplace for new and used goods.
- [Walmart Marketplace](https://marketplace.walmart.com) - Walmart's marketplace for sellers to reach its online shoppers.

## Email Marketing & Reviews

- [Klaviyo](https://www.klaviyo.com) - Ecommerce email and SMS marketing platform with segmentation and flows.
- [Omnisend](https://www.omnisend.com) - Email, SMS, and push marketing automation built for online stores.
- [Judge.me](https://judge.me) - Product review app with photo reviews, Q&A, and Google Shopping integration.
- [Okendo](https://www.okendo.io) - Review and UGC platform for collecting and displaying social proof.
- [Yotpo](https://www.yotpo.com) - Reviews, loyalty, and SMS marketing suite for ecommerce brands.
- [Loox](https://www.loox.io) - Photo-review app that turns customer photos into storefront social proof.

## Dropshipping

- [DSers](https://www.dsers.com) - AliExpress dropshipping and order-fulfillment tool for Shopify stores.
- [Spocket](https://www.spocket.co) - Dropshipping marketplace with fast shipping from US and EU suppliers.
- [Zendrop](https://www.zendrop.com) - Dropshipping platform with automated fulfillment and one-click product import.

## Subscriptions & Recurring Billing

- [Recharge](https://rechargepayments.com) - Subscription management and recurring billing platform for Shopify and BigCommerce.
- [Bold](https://boldcommerce.com) - Subscription, cart, and checkout tools for ecommerce merchants.

## Print on Demand

- [Printful](https://www.printful.com) - Print-on-demand fulfillment for custom apparel, home goods, and accessories.
- [Printify](https://printify.com) - Print-on-demand marketplace connecting merchants with a global network of print providers.

## Analytics & Insights

- [Matomo](https://matomo.org) - Open-source web analytics platform with ecommerce tracking.
- [PostHog](https://posthog.com) - Open-source product analytics, session recording, and feature flags.
- [Google Analytics 4](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/) - Google's event-based analytics platform with ecommerce reports.
- [Triple Whale](https://www.triplewhale.com) - Ecommerce analytics dashboard consolidating ad spend, sales, and profitability.
- [FlipWorth](https://flipworth.silentdirectivellc.com/?utm_source=awesome-ecommerce&utm_medium=resource-directory) - Photo-based resale price research for secondhand sellers: snap an item and get an estimated resale range, a buy or pass verdict, a suggested list price and where to sell it. Free to try in the browser, also on iPhone.

## Content Management

- [SanityCMS](https://www.sanity.io) - Headless content platform for structured product content and editorial workflows.
- [WordPress](https://wordpress.org) - Open-source CMS powering a large share of ecommerce sites via WooCommerce.
